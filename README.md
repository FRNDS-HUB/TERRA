# TERRA — Every Life Matters

*Every life matters. Every mind can learn.*

TERRA is an independent educational platform concept focused on human resilience, disaster preparedness, accessible learning, and environmental awareness.

## Mission

To make preparedness knowledge, educational resources, and environmental awareness easier to discover in one accessible platform.

## Features

- Interactive emergency preparedness checklist
- Searchable and filterable learning resources
- Interactive lesson details
- Knowledge quiz with feedback and retry functionality
- Links to established information sources, including IFRC, UNESCO, UNEP, and WHO
- Responsive desktop and mobile interface

## Technology

TERRA is an independent educational project, not an emergency response service or a substitute for official local instructions or professional advice.
